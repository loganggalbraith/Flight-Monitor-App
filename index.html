How the tracker works

Destination Identified

A flyExclusive flight has route information showing an airport in the selected state or region as its destination. The aircraft may still be far outside that state when it first appears.

Possible Approach

If a confirmed destination is unavailable, the tracker compares the aircraft's position and heading with monitored private-jet airports in the selected state or region. This is an estimate, not a confirmed destination.

Departure Identified

A flyExclusive aircraft is on the ground near a monitored airport and route information identifies that airport as the origin with another airport as its destination.

Repositioning Opportunity Score

The tracker can estimate the likelihood of another departure after an aircraft lands. This is a prediction only; it does not confirm that a flight is empty, available for booking, or available through Vaunt.

Discord Notifications

The website tracks all states, but Discord notifications remain limited to Southern California.
