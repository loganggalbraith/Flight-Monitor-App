How alerts work

National Dashboard Tracking

The web dashboard can display detected flyExclusive aircraft across the United States and organize them by destination or current region.

Southern California Discord Alerts

Discord remains intentionally limited to Southern California. Flights elsewhere in the country can appear on the dashboard without generating a Discord notification.

Possible Approach

When a confirmed destination is unavailable, the tracker can compare an aircraft's position and heading with nearby monitored airports. This is an estimate, not a confirmed destination.

Grounded / Reposition Watch

An aircraft observed on the ground near a monitored airport can remain visible so its next outbound route can be watched. A likely reposition is an estimate, not confirmation of an empty leg or Vaunt availability.
