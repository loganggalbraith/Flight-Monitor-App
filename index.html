How alerts work

California Destination Identified

A flyExclusive flight has route information showing a California airport as its destination. The aircraft may still be outside California when this alert appears.

Possible Approach

No California destination has been identified from the route data, but the aircraft is already inside California and its current position and heading suggest it may be approaching one of the monitored SoCal airports. This is an estimate, not a confirmed destination.

SoCal Departure Identified

A flyExclusive aircraft is currently on the ground at a monitored SoCal airport, and route information identifies that airport as the origin with another airport as its destination.
